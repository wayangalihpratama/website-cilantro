From Morning Coffee & Breakfast to Late Night Margaritas

At Cilantro Ubud, we open early at 8:00 AM to serve fresh roasted Balinese coffee, Huevos Rancheros, and hearty Breakfast Burritos before transitioning into our signature Mexican lunch and evening cantina experience.

Whether you are enjoying a peaceful brunch in Nyuh Kuning, meeting friends for happy hour cocktails, or looking for Ubud’s best vegan jackfruit tacos with housemade cashew cheese, our welcoming team is ready to serve you.
