Where Mexican Passion Meets the Serenity of Ubud

At Cilantro Ubud, we believe great Mexican food is built on genuine ingredients: slow simmering, freshly ground chillies, hand-pressed tortillas, and love.

Whether you're unwinding after exploring the Monkey Forest, craving cheesy quesadillas with friends, or searching for Ubud’s best vegan jackfruit tacos with housemade cashew cheese, Cilantro offers a relaxed, welcoming sanctuary for all food lovers.
